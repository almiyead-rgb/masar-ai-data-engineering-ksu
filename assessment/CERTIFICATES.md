# Assessment and certificate policy

This policy applies to this course. It is not a statement of a general SDAIA certification policy.

| Attendance | Project result | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| All five days | Assessed score of **70–100 out of 100** | **Completion/pass certificate** |
| All five days | Assessed score below 70 | **Attendance certificate only** |
| All five days | No project submitted | **Attendance certificate only** |
| All five days | Submitted, not assessed yet | **Pending assessment** |
| Fewer than five days | Any project result | Does not meet the stated completion requirement; ask the organizer about attendance-certificate eligibility |

**A score of 70 is included in the passing range.** Attendance adds no points to the project score. Keep “not submitted” separate from a submitted project awarded zero, even though both lead to attendance-only certification when all five days were attended.

The instructor confirms the official attendance process, treatment of late arrival/early departure, final deadline and any resubmission rule. Personal progress checkboxes on the website do not establish attendance or certificate entitlement.
