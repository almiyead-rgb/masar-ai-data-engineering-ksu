# Lab evidence notes

[Course home](https://github.com/almiyead-rgb/masar-ai-data-engineering-ksu)

Notebook setup creates your working copy under `student/` and preserves existing notes. Fill it with your own observed evidence before the daily save. An unchanged template is not completed evidence.

## Identity

Record lab number, run date, code commit/version, environment and dataset manifest hash.

## What ran

Name the notebook and cells/scripts executed. State any missing dependency or skipped section explicitly.

## Evidence and interpretation

Link the saved output and explain what it proves, what it does not prove and the row/grain definition.

## Rerun and recovery

Record repeat-run comparisons and distinguish content equality from changing run timestamps.

## Decision and next step

Explain your decision and one observed issue or limitation; do not fill this template with invented PASS results.
