Notebook setup creates your working copy under `student/` and preserves existing notes. Fill it with your own observed evidence before the daily save. An unchanged template is not completed evidence.

## Dataset and ownership

Identify the synthetic dataset, steward role, source path and permitted training use; do not include real personal contact details.

## Lineage and access

Map inputs, transformations and outputs. Explain intended access for each layer and separate raw identifiers from BI outputs.

## Quality and retention

Link quality/quarantine evidence and record the training retention/erasure procedure and its limits. This is not legal advice or a compliance certificate.
