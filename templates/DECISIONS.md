# Architecture decision log

[Course home](https://github.com/almiyead-rgb/masar-ai-data-engineering-ksu)

Notebook setup creates your working copy under `student/` and preserves existing notes. Fill it with your own observed evidence before the daily save. An unchanged template is not completed evidence.

## Decision

State the choice, context and alternative considered.

## Reason and evidence

Connect the choice to a course outcome and a measured observation or explicit assumption.

## Consequences

State complexity, cost, reliability and privacy trade-offs, plus conditions that would change the decision.
