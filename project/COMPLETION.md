Use this to inspect your package. It helps you prepare a reviewable submission; it is not an automatic grade.

- [ ] I attended the course through the organizer's official attendance process.
- [ ] I saved each daily notebook with my own execution outputs.
- [ ] I kept the fixed synthetic sources unchanged.
- [ ] I explained the business problem and architecture choice.
- [ ] I retained the Bronze/Silver, dbt and Delta evidence.
- [ ] I retained the Kafka, quality, quarantine and governance evidence.
- [ ] I inspected the eight final products and reconciled their measures.
- [ ] I kept unknown future labels as NULL/UNOBSERVED.
- [ ] I saved all five daily answers, including any corrections after discussion.
- [ ] My `student/PROJECT_README.md` explains the run order and actual limitations, and my root `README.md` links to it.
- [ ] My GitHub repository opens at the branch and commit I am submitting.
- [ ] I retained a complete handoff archive for recovery or review.
- [ ] I included the programme, instructor attribution, Academy link and hashtag.
- [ ] I used the announced private channel and deadline for final submission.
